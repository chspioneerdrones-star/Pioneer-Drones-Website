About the team

We're a brand-new team building our first competition drone from scratch.

Pioneer Drones just started at Conestoga High School. This page tracks who we are, what we're building toward, and how the team is organized as we get off the ground.

How we started

Pioneer Drones was founded by three students at Conestoga High School who wanted to compete in UAS4STEM. We're early in the process: figuring out our airframe, splitting up responsibilities, and learning as we build. Nothing here is inherited from a bigger program — this is our team, built from the ground up.

Where we are right now

We're in the planning and prototyping phase. Our current focus is finalizing the frame design, setting up flight electronics, and getting our first test flights on the books before the season ramps up.

How the team is organized

Three people, three areas of ownership

Programming

Flight logic, control software, and any tools we build for testing and operations.

Electrical

Power distribution, component layout, wiring, and the reliability details that keep the drone airworthy.

Design

Frame geometry, component placement, printed or machined parts, and iteration after physical testing.

What's next

Our near-term goals

Finish the first airframe prototype and get it flight-ready.

Run our first real test flights and log what we learn.

Bring on sponsors and supporters to help cover parts and travel costs.

Post regular updates here as the team grows and the build progresses.
