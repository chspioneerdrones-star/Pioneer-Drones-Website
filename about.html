About us

We're a brand-new team building our first competition drone.

Pioneer Drones started at Conestoga High School with three students who wanted to compete in UAS4STEM. This page tracks who we are and how we're organized as we build.

How we started

We founded Pioneer Drones because we wanted to build something real and compete in UAS4STEM. We're early in the process — figuring out our airframe, splitting up responsibilities, and learning as we go. This is our team, built entirely from the ground up.

Where we are now

We're in the planning and prototyping phase. Right now we're finalizing our frame design, setting up flight electronics, and getting ready for our first test flights before the season ramps up.

How we're organized

Three people, three areas of ownership

Programming

We write the flight logic, control software, and any tools we need for testing and operations.

Electrical

We handle power distribution, component layout, wiring, and the reliability details that keep the drone airworthy.

Design

We design the frame geometry, place components, and iterate after every physical test.

What's next

Our near-term goals

Finish our first airframe prototype and get it flight-ready.

Run our first real test flights and log what we learn.

Bring on sponsors and supporters to help cover parts and travel costs.

Post regular updates here as our team grows and the build progresses.
