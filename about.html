About the team

We want the website to sound like an engineering team, not a sci-fi ad.

This page explains what Pioneer Drones is, how the team is structured, and what kind of work visitors should expect to see as the season progresses.

Who we are

Pioneer Drones is a student-led effort at Conestoga High School focused on building and improving drones for UAS4STEM. The team combines programming, design, and electrical work instead of treating the drone like a single black-box product.

Why this redesign works better

The old version leaned on generic hype language. This version explains real responsibilities, concrete goals, and how support connects to actual team progress.

How the team works

Clear responsibilities make student projects feel credible.

Programming

Flight logic, control software, and whatever tools the team builds for testing and operations.

Electrical

Power distribution, component layout, wiring decisions, and the reliability details that keep a drone airworthy.

Design

Frame geometry, component placement, printed or machined parts, and iteration after physical testing.
