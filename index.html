Pioneer Drones

Conestoga UAS4STEM Team

Berwyn, Pennsylvania

Three students building a competition drone from the ground up.

We're a new team at Conestoga High School preparing for UAS4STEM. Every part of the aircraft — frame, wiring, and flight software — is being designed, built, and tested by us.

Meet the team Support us

Our approach

We build in the open.

We're documenting our drone's development as it happens: frame revisions, wiring decisions, and what we learn from every test flight. Nothing here is polished for show — it's the actual state of the build.

Right now

We're finalizing our airframe, wiring up flight electronics, and preparing for our first real test flights ahead of the UAS4STEM season.

What we're working on

The three parts of the build

Frame design

We're iterating on the airframe through CAD and physical prototypes, adjusting after every test based on what breaks or underperforms.

Flight software

We're writing the control logic and autonomy tools that keep the drone stable and on-mission.

Electronics

We're selecting, wiring, and mounting every component so the aircraft holds up under real flight conditions.

Our timeline
